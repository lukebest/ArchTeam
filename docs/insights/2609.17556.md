## 四视角独立批评

### 微架构

Pretrained ViT split channel-wise into two physically isolated subnetworks. Four runtime modes: Full-Precision (FP), Low-Power (LP), High-Reliability (HR), Adaptive (AD); mode scheduling + resource management claimed to keep inference uninterrupted; transitions “within few clock cycles.” Hardware: lightweight GPU-like FPGA accelerator (PERUN as an FGPU extension) plus a “minimal runtime support layer” for mode scheduling and resource allocation; authors say no extensive datapath rewrite. Continual learning restricted to empirically fault-resilient parameter regions; reliability-critical parameters held. ViT structure restated: H×W×C → N P×P patches → D-dim + CLS + posenc → L encoder blocks; each block MHSA then GELU MLP, each preceded by LayerNorm and wrapped in additive residuals; residuals strictly additive, no inter-block gate/re-norm; LN twice per block (MHSA + MLP) plus once at output; γ/β per-channel, shared across tokens. Subnet internals (how channels are split, what HR vs LP actually clocks) are not drawn on p.1–3.

### 负载

Mission-critical edge, especially satellite onboard intelligence (disaster/wildfire/maritime/autonomous nav): eclipse kills solar, radiation varies by orbital region, observation distribution drifts with cycle/illumination/sensor aging, no manual intervention. Also named: autonomous systems, industrial monitoring. SEUs flip on-chip stored parameters; fault probability is time-varying. ViT fault-injection literature cited: most bit faults benign; a small subset emits NaN/Inf that invalidates the whole stream. Continual learning vs uninterrupted inference is the second load axis.

### 仿真方法学

Abstract: network-level failure rate “only 1.79% under high Bit Error Rates”; hardware “<5% area overhead”; mode transitions in “few clock cycles.” No BER value, no dataset, no FPGA part, no area breakdown on p.1–3. Eval promised in unread §V (reliability, adaptation, deployment). Treat 1.79% / <5% / few cycles as unpaid until those sections.

### 工业可行性

Hook is a runtime layer on an existing programmable FPGA GPU-like engine rather than a new PE. Channel-wise isolation + four modes is the product story for COTS-in-orbit (cheaper/faster than rad-hard). Adoption needs a mode policy that tracks power/radiation/drift without a ground loop, and a CL update that never touches the fragile parameter region. Static pipelines in current edge accelerators are the admitted barrier. Area <5% and “few cycles” are still abstract claims. No process node or power number on p.1–3.

## Master Class

1. **The Real Abstract** — Orbit (and other long-running edges) change power, radiation, and data at once; a ViT that cannot switch protection/precision without stopping inference will either waste joules in the quiet or die in the storm.

2. **The Rashomon Synthesis** — Reliability people want TMR always-on; CL people want to pause and fine-tune; accelerator people ship static pipelines; ViT residuals and shared LN γ/β turn one SEU into a batch-wide NaN. Isolation, mode, and update-scope are one problem.

3. **The Magic Trick** — Split a pretrained ViT channel-wise into two physically isolated subnets; schedule FP/LP/HR/AD on a thin FPGA runtime; confine online updates to empirically resilient parameter regions.

4. **The Skeleton in the Closet** — 1.79% / <5% / few cycles have no method on p.1–3; what “channel-wise” and each mode actually gate is unspecified here; CL restricted to resilient regions may not be enough to track orbital drift; two subnets still share the residual stream’s numerical fate if isolation is incomplete.

5. **可提取的问题线索**（仅 p.1–3，有数字，无机制名）

1. 卫星等任务关键边缘场景在轨穿越不同功耗、辐射与环境区间：日食期太阳能消失、辐射强度跨轨道区显著变化，观测分布还随周期、光照与传感器老化漂移，且无法人工干预。(p.1)
2. 高能粒子诱发 SEU，使片上存储的网络参数在推理中被改写；故障概率随时间连续变化——全程套同一套保护会在低风险期付出多余计算与能耗，在高辐射期又可能不够。(p.1–2)
3. ViT 故障注入表明：多数比特级故障功能上无害，但一小部分会沿残差与共享表示流传播出 NaN/Inf，使整条计算流失效，置信度估计或后处理无法挽救；常规 TMR 等又假设静态工况与固定开销。(p.2)
4. 持续学习若要停推理更新参数，会打断安全/任务关键决策；若在适配期间发生瞬态故障，错误还会经梯度更新永久污染模型；现有方法多忽视“不中断推理 + 在线适配期并发硬件故障”。(p.2)
5. ViT 编码器块残差严格相加，LayerNorm 在每个块内对 MHSA 与 MLP 各做一次（块内两次）且 γ/β 跨 token 共享，单点故障可一致放大到整批；现有边缘加速器又多为静态流水与固定资源分配，难在不中断推理下切换执行模式。(p.2–3)
