## 四视角独立批评

### 微架构

Claimed SoC: RISC-V host plus an 8×8 mesh CGRA and 64 KB data memory (Fig. 2 shows four 8 KB DM blocks at the edges). PE (Fig. 3): SRAM configuration memory (64-bit × 32 instructions), 16-bit ALU (18 arithmetic/logic ops named), control/decode, 6-input / 7-output crossbar router, and a buffer-less one-cycle bypass to a distant PE. Enhancement is a configurable MAC plus truncation in the PE: the same multiplier and adder, muxed by opcode, do ADD / MUL / MAC; MAC keeps a 32-bit accum register (2× the 16-bit inputs). Truncation (Fig. 4) runs after all accumulations; boundary-check overflow clips to all-ones; four instruction-selected fixed-point modes (e.g. FXP⟨8,8⟩, ⟨6,10⟩, ⟨4,12⟩, ⟨2,14⟩). Conventional mapping is two instructions / two cycles (MUL then ADD, possibly on a neighbor). Proposed mapping completes MAC in one instruction / one cycle inside the PE (Fig. 5). Path delay of the MAC is reported ~3.2 ns, 1.86× the multiplier-only path; authors say 100 MHz target still holds. Synthesized PE area 0.019 mm² in 40 nm; Fig. 7 layout caption also prints “28 nm | ~0.01 mm²”—treat that caption/text mismatch as unresolved on p.1–3.

### 负载

Edge devices that concurrently run image recognition, voice, and other dynamic real-time tasks. Positioning (Fig. 1): CGRA between CPU flexibility and ASIC/FPGA/DSP efficiency, under a “power wall” that forces energy over raw performance. MAC-dense kernels called out: neural-network inference and FFT; GeMM is the named efficiency example (up to 37% overall CGRA energy-efficiency improvement). Compiler maps PE ops and neighbor routes; multiple PEs execute in parallel. Truncation-mode choices are motivated by cited CNN/MNIST / 16-bit-weight fractional-length analysis, not by a new accuracy sweep on these pages.

### 仿真方法学

Abstract/intro numbers: 40 nm CMOS, 420.6 GOPS/W at 0.6 V / 21 MHz, 1.4× the stated SOTA; GeMM energy-efficiency “up to 37%.” p.3 starts PE evaluation: Verilog, 40 nm synthesis, 0.019 mm², Fig. 6 power/area breakdown, Fig. 7 layout, 3.2 ns / 1.86× path-delay note, 100 MHz target. Full array efficiency tables, workload set, and the SOTA comparison setup are unread. Treat 420.6 GOPS/W / 1.4× / 37% as author-reported. The 21 MHz efficiency point and the 100 MHz timing claim are different operating corners—do not collapse them.

### 工业可行性

Story is a drop-in PE upgrade on a conventional edge CGRA (RISC-V host, 8×8 mesh, shared DM) rather than a new array topology. Single-cycle MAC without giving up ADD/MUL opcodes is the product hook; the timing tax is the longer 3.2 ns MAC path. 420.6 GOPS/W is quoted at 0.6 V / 21 MHz—an efficiency corner, not a 4K-video or phone-NPU frequency. 40 nm + 64 KB DM is plausible IP, but compiler retuning, bypass-path occupancy, and whether 16-bit + 4-mode truncation covers the real edge mix are not evidenced on p.1–3. Fig. 7’s 28 nm caption vs 40 nm text is a documentation risk if anyone copies the layout number.

## Master Class

1. **The Real Abstract** — Edge CGRAs already sit in the flexibility/efficiency gap, but a MAC that takes two instructions stretches NN/FFT/GeMM; fuse ADD/MUL/MAC onto one multiplier-adder in a single cycle and truncate the wide accum on the way out.

2. **The Rashomon Synthesis** — SoC people hit the CPU power wall; CGRA people sell reconfigurability; kernel people count MACs that currently cost two PE cycles; precision people need a 32-bit accum that still talks 16-bit to neighbors and DM.

3. **The Magic Trick** — Opcode-mux the same signed multiplier and adder for ADD, MUL, or MAC; keep a 32-bit accum; after the last accumulate, boundary-check and shift/truncate into one of four 16-bit fixed-point readouts.

4. **The Skeleton in the Closet** — 420.6 GOPS/W is at 21 MHz / 0.6 V, unread as a full-array eval; MAC path is 1.86× slower than MUL; Fig. 7 caption disagrees with the 40 nm / 0.019 mm² text; MNIST-cited truncation modes are not a general accuracy argument on these pages.

5. **可提取的问题线索**（仅 p.1–3，有数字，无机制名）

1. 边缘设备同时要跑识别、语音等动态实时负载，通用核撞上功耗墙，需要灵活又高效的加速底物。(p.1)
2. 典型 CGRA 把 MAC 拆成 MUL+ADD 两条指令，至少两拍才能完成一次乘加；神经网络与 FFT 等 MAC 密集负载因此被拉长处理时间。(p.1)
3. 在 GeMM 类负载上，两拍 MAC 映射直接抬高周期数与能耗；需要在不牺牲可重构灵活性的前提下压低 MAC 路径周期。(p.1)
