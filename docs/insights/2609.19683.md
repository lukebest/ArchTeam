## 四视角独立批评

### 微架构

Not a new systolic topology—authors invert the number format and then map the dual-format product onto a shifter-based PE. Standard MX: xi = 2^{Eblock} × mi, one shared exponent, per-element low-bit mantissa (MXINT / MXFP), default block B=32. NVFP4 (B=16) adds nested scales: xi = Stensor × Sblock × mi (tensor FP32 × block FP8). MiX inverts the grouping: private per-element exponents under a single shared block mantissa, so the bit budget goes to intra-block range rather than per-element significands. Adaptive dual-format (MiX-MX): MiX-formatted activation tensor paired with an MX-formatted weight tensor; the shared MiX mantissa factors out of the block dot product, which is the algebraic excuse for deleting per-element MACs. Datapath claim: bit-shifters + integer adder trees on a custom systolic accelerator. Related PE costs named as the thing being avoided: mixed-precision multipliers or dynamic unscale-to-FP16 (W4A16); MicroScopiQ irregular dataflows / dequant trees / silicon-heavy multipliers; MXFP4+ / AMXFP4 enlarged PEs (extended-mantissa block max or two asymmetric FP8 scales). Implementation sentence: MiX accelerator and baselines in TSMC28 CMOS. Exact PE array size, shifter width, adder-tree depth, and the runtime MiX-vs-MX policy are not drawn on p.1–3.

### 负载

Edge VLM inference: vision encoder → modality projector → LLM backbone; prefill concatenates high-density vision tokens with discrete text embeddings. Dual memory wall: model weights plus a KV cache grown by “tens of thousands” of high-resolution visual tokens. Weight-only W4A16 leaves visual KV and dynamic activations uncompressed. Empirical ranges on these pages: visual activations about −20 to 10; text tokens concentrated near zero. Qwen2-VL-7B on OCRBench (invoice text): mean |activation| μv=0.312 vs μt=0.0091 (~34× channel dynamic-range gap); vision 99th-percentile outliers >2.07, text rarely >0.061 (medians 0.1956 vs 0.0055 on Fig. 1). Attention matrices: Q/K show persistent channel-wise vertical spikes that grow with depth; V shows early token-wise horizontal spikes. Fig. 3, Layer-27 Key of Qwen2-VL-7B, B=32: background-token fatal underflow 44.8% (MXINT4), 38.4% (MXFP4), 38.3% (NVFP4 at B=32 despite nested scaling); authors report MiX at 23.9% on the same map. Named unread eval models: Qwen2-VL, LLaVA-OneVision, MiniCPM-V; six VLM benchmarks on LLaVA-OneVision-7B and MiniCPM-V-2.6. Token-pruning accelerators (Focus et al.) are cited as skipping visual tokens without compressing weights.

### 仿真方法学

Abstract / end of intro: 4.5-bit MiX “equivalent or superior” to NVFP4 on multi-modal benchmarks; PE area efficiency +25% vs NVFP4; 2.3–4.5× speedup and 1.4–2.9× energy reduction vs Focus across models / six VLM benchmarks. Measurement fabric, array dimensions, and full tables are unread. Fig. 1–3 on these pages are activation / QKV / underflow characterization, not accelerator timing. Treat 25% / 2.3–4.5× / 1.4–2.9× and the NVFP4-accuracy claim as unpaid until unread plots. 34×, μv/μt, p99, and the 44.8 / 38.4 / 38.3 / 23.9% underflow rates are author-stated p.1–3 characterization.

### 工业可行性

Story is a format + PE substitution under an edge area/power envelope: keep a block bit budget comparable to MX, spend it on per-element exponents, and delete the 16-bit / mixed-precision multiplier. TSMC28 is named; no PE mm² or memory-system budget on p.1–3. Dual-format mapping assumes MX weights stay well-behaved while activations take the inverted format—the adaptive policy that chooses when to do that is named, not specified. Residual 23.9% “dead” on the Layer-27 Key map is still a large hole. W4A16 and NVFP4 nested scales are the industrial baselines being argued against, not a shipping compiler story.

## Master Class

1. **The Real Abstract** — Edge VLMs need sub-8-bit end-to-end, but a shared block exponent dies when vision outliers sit next to near-zero text. Invert the format so each element keeps its own exponent, share one mantissa, and the PE can shift instead of multiply.

2. **The Rashomon Synthesis** — Quant people share one scale per block; VLM people see a ~34× vision/text gap plus channel-wise Q/K spikes; PE people still pay mixed-precision multiply or unscale-to-FP16. The hijacked shared exponent and the 16-bit multiplier are the same bottleneck.

3. **The Magic Trick** — Group private per-element exponents under one shared mantissa; pair MiX activations with MX weights so that mantissa factors out of the block dot product; replace the PE multiply with shifters and an integer adder tree.

4. **The Skeleton in the Closet** — 25% area / 2.3–4.5× / 1.4–2.9× have no setup on p.1–3; 23.9% dead-zone is still a large residual; dual-format mapping and the adaptive policy are named, not scheduled; shifter-PE area vs NVFP4 is unpaid silicon; TSMC28 array size unstated.

5. **可提取的问题线索**（仅 p.1–3，有数字，无机制名）

1. 端侧部署 VLM 严重受内存带宽制约，需要激进的亚 8-bit 量化；多模态 token 的动态范围差距会使标准块缩放格式出现「共享指数被离群值劫持、邻域下溢、注意力图被毁」一类的塌缩。(p.1)
2. 工业常见仅权重量化（如 W4A16）对 VLM 不够：视觉侧 KV 与动态激活足迹仍未压缩；推理单元要么用昂贵混合精度乘，要么把权重量化回 FP16，面积与动态功耗仍绑在 16-bit 逻辑上。(p.1)
3. 经验剖面显示视觉激活可跨约 −20 到 10，文本 token 则紧贴零附近；同质量化尺度要么猛裁视觉离群，要么逼文本细粒度下溢。(p.1)
4. 在 Qwen2-VL-7B 的 OCRBench 剖面中，视觉 token 平均幅度约 0.312、文本约 0.0091，约 34× 通道动态范围差；视觉 99 分位离群可超约 2.07，文本同百分位少超约 0.061。(p.2)
5. 在块大小 32 时，共享尺度格式下 Layer-27 Key 矩阵背景 token 致命下溢可达约 44.8%（MXINT4）与约 38.4%（MXFP4）；即便更紧的嵌套缩放（块 32）仍约 38.3% 下溢，多模态信号在离群污染区大量丢失。(p.2–3)
