## 四视角独立批评

### 微架构

Serialized but flip-frontier recompute; spin-select queue; priority weight banking; shot-level temp control skips idle; 16:1 RNG amortize. Not a new spin cell—update sparsity architecture.

### 负载

Fully connected MAX-CUT / Ising; N~2000–2048; COP embeddings that hate sparse graphs.

### 仿真方法学

Post-layout sim only (explicitly not silicon); compares to projected STATICA and GPU SA (52.8 W). Validation methodology gap acknowledged on p.1–2.

### 工业可行性

28nm, 3×4 mm; power 0.15 mW/spin attractive vs GPU, but post-layout≠chip; dense weight SRAM (16 Mb) and N² connectivity still dominate productization.

## Master Class

1. **The Real Abstract** — Keep full connectivity on a serial dense annealer by only recomputing interactions for spins that actually flip.

2. **The Rashomon Synthesis** — Dense COP wants N² fidelity; silicon wants mux/power down; latency wants parallel but area explodes past hundreds of spins.

3. **The Magic Trick** — Differential update turns per-step work into active-frontier sparsity instead of full-spin sweeps (up to ~30.6× fewer SSA cycles claimed).

4. **The Skeleton in the Closet** — All headline TTS/energy are post-layout; prior ASIC baselines are often silicon—comparison caveat is stated but easy to over-read.

5. **可提取的问题线索**（仅 p.1–3，有数字，无机制名）

1. 全连接退火器要么每步提供 N² 权重带宽，要么串行扫完全部 N 个自旋；N 到数千时周期、面积与功耗一起膨胀。(p.1)
2. 近期全连接数字退火器约 512–576 自旋、亚 400 MHz，即便多片分区仍耗数百毫瓦量级。(p.1)
3. 对全连接设计，N² 串行化使每次退火（one shot）延迟到数千周期量级；并行数据通路要 N 个多路选择与每步 N² 权重读取，数百自旋以上就难以承受。(p.1)
4. 稠密约 2,000 自旋图：不论实际翻转多少，每个温度步大约仍要约 2,000 个周期。(p.1–2)
5. 当代 GPU 上 SA 基线约 52.8 W、单次退火数百毫秒量级，与专用退火器存在约三个数量级的功耗差距叙述。(p.2)
