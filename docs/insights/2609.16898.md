## 四视角独立批评

### 微架构

Not a new HE PE. Authors attach a commercial FPGA HE accelerator (cited) whose organization is described as F1/ARK-like: HBM, on-chip scratchpad, multiple compute clusters with NTTU / AutoU / ModMultU / ModAddU. Fig. 4(b) labels 16 GB HBM, 25 MB SRAM, 10 GB/s SRAM / 256 GB/s HBM bandwidth, 150 W. Client and server CPUs talk over a wireless network; ciphertexts then cross PCIe onto the server accelerator. The claimed novelty is (1) a convolution protocol that emits fewer output ciphertexts packed with useful coefficients instead of dummy-heavy polynomials, (2) compress sparse coefficient-form weight plaintexts and decompress / convert to evaluation form on the fly, (3) reorder HE ops so a working-set subset is fully consumed in the scratchpad before writeback. Authors call the contributions hardware-agnostic. Exact cluster counts, on-the-fly convert cost, and the new protocol’s op mix are after p.3.

### 负载

Two-party private DNN inference: client owns the input, server owns the model; hybrid HE on linear layers (convolutions) and MPC on nonlinearities (ReLU; CrypTFlow2 named). Applications listed: medical diagnosis, face recognition, finance. End-to-end example is ImageNet-scale ResNet-50 (Fig. 1) plus later ResNet-18/50 latency claims. Coefficient encoding (vs SIMD) is chosen as MPC-friendly; polynomial multiply implements convolution, but dummy coefficients dominate—Fig. 3 example puts two useful results across two ciphertexts that could have held eight coefficients. After the protocol rewrite, server-side pressure shifts to weight plaintexts (coefficient form ~128 KB vs evaluation form tens–hundreds of MB; Fig. 2 also marks 64 MB / 128 MB) and to intermediate ciphertexts whose working set exceeds the scratchpad. Weights in coefficient form are described as >99% zero-padded; evaluation-form conversion inflates size by “over 1000×.”

### 仿真方法学

p.1–3 are motivation + encoding background. Fig. 1: individual HE ops 182× / 444× on the accelerator; Cheetah end-to-end only ~1.37× (and a 2.15× LAN bar is drawn); linear-layer time after accel is dominated by output NetIO. Abstract/intro also claim ≤5.7× (CPU) and 4.2× (with accelerator) vs Cheetah, and ImageNet ResNet-18 / ResNet-50 at 2.9 s / 14.6 s. Fig. 4(c) is per-op latency at N=8192, log q ≈ 64. Measurement fabric, WAN/LAN models, and full tables are unread. Treat 1.37× / 5.7× / 4.2× / 10× traffic / 2.9 s / 14.6 s as author-reported.

### 工业可行性

Product is a protocol + compression + schedule overlay on an already-shipping HE FPGA, not a new crypto ASIC. That is the adoption path—and the limit: wireless ciphertext NetIO still sits outside the chip, PCIe still feeds HBM, and 2.9 s / 14.6 s ImageNet is not interactive. Honest-but-curious 2PC, public architecture/tensor shapes, BFV/LHE (no bootstrapping story on these pages). Hardware-agnostic claim means the same communication/memory problems reappear on F1/ARK-class parts. No new silicon area on p.1–3.

## Master Class

1. **The Real Abstract** — HE accelerators already win the single operator by orders of magnitude; hybrid private inference still dies on wireless output-ciphertext traffic. Change the convolution protocol so fewer, denser ciphertexts go over the air, then keep the extra server-side plaintexts and temporaries from thrashing DRAM.

2. **The Rashomon Synthesis** — Crypto-accelerator people show 100×-class op speedups; protocol people still ship a ciphertext pair per linear layer; memory people see coefficient-sparse weights explode in evaluation form; interconnect people watch output NetIO own the linear-layer timeline once compute is cheap.

3. **The Magic Trick** — Encode convolutions so output ciphertexts carry mostly useful coefficients; store weights compressed in sparse coefficient form and convert on the fly; schedule so an intermediate-ciphertext subset is fully drained from the scratchpad before eviction.

4. **The Skeleton in the Closet** — 5.7× / 4.2× / 2.9 s / 14.6 s are unread beyond the opening; the new protocol is admitted to create more HE compute and a larger temporary working set; 10× traffic cut is a compression claim, not a closed WAN budget; “hardware-agnostic” also means no new PE to hide HBM/PCIe.

5. **可提取的问题线索**（仅 p.1–3，有数字，无机制名）

1. 混合 HE-MPC 隐私推理里，线性层走 HE、非线性走 MPC；HE 算子加速器对单算子可有数量级加速，但接到 SOTA 协议端到端往往只剩约 1.37×。(p.1, Fig.1)
2. 端到端收益被网络通信吃掉：每个 HE 算子常要无线传输输入/输出密文，输出 NetIO 在算力加速后仍可主导线性层时延。(p.1, Fig.1c–d)
3. 为少传输出密文而改写协议后，服务器侧出现大量权重明文与中间密文，DRAM 与片上容量之间的访存压力上升（明文可从约 128KB 系数形态膨胀到数十–上百 MB 求值形态量级）。(p.2)
4. 中间密文工作集大于片上 scratchpad 时，复用差会导致频繁片外访问，形成与通信瓶颈并列的第二类部署障碍。(p.2)
5. 旧协议输出密文含大量哑元系数，有用系数占比低，放大无线传输体积。(p.2)
